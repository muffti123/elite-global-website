    Passengers

    Add every traveller, including yourself if you are travelling. Each passenger needs their passport biodata page (PDF, JPG or PNG, PDF up to 5 MB) and a passport photograph (25–50 KB — larger photos are resized automatically on your device). On a phone you can take the pictures with your camera. A flight ticket and hotel booking are optional.

    Passport photo guide — what a good photo looks like
    • A recent photo
    • Plain white background
    • Face centred, looking at the camera
    • Portrait shape (35 × 45 mm), at least 200 × 250 pixels
    • No selfies taken at arm's length
    • No full-body or group shots
    • Correct

    • Busy background
