FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how our enquiry process works.

What is the difference between “Visa Only” and “Customize My Umrah Experience”?

Choose Request Umrah Visa Only if you only need help with your Umrah visa. Choose Customize My Umrah Experience if you would also like us to arrange flights, hotels in Makkah and Madinah, transport or a complete package. Our team will prepare a proposal based on your preferences. Both open a short page here on our website.

Do I need my passport to make an enquiry?

For Request Umrah Visa Only, yes: we need a passport biodata page and a passport photograph for every traveller to start your application. To Customize My Umrah Experience or simply ask a question, you don't need your passport. Send your enquiry first and we'll collect passports before visa processing begins.

How do I upload passports and photos? Do I need to sign in?

No sign-in is needed. On our Request Umrah Visa Only page you add each traveller's passport biodata page (PDF, JPG or PNG) and a passport photograph directly on our website. Photos must be between 25 KB and 50 KB; larger photos are resized automatically on your device, and photos under 25 KB are rejected so you can choose a clearer one. Your files go straight to Elite Global and are stored privately, so only authorised staff can see them.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

A member of the Elite Global team reviews your request and contacts you by phone or WhatsApp to confirm your details, explain the next steps and, for customised trips, discuss options and pricing. You'll see a reference number (e.g. EG-VO-00012); please quote it when we contact you.

Can you arrange travel for families and groups?

Yes. Tell us how many adults, children and infants are travelling, and we will tailor the arrangements to your group. On the visa application you can add up to 50 travellers.

Ready to begin your Umrah journey?

Tell us what you need. Our team will get in touch to guide you through the next steps.
